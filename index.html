Audio Cheat Sheet

Sound check, gain, HPF and EQ. Starting points, not presets. Trust your ears.

Sound check

  1. Start muted. Unmute only the channel you are checking, +48V only for condensers. Have fader all the way down to -∞.
  2. [SEL] the channel and have the player play at full performance level.
  3. Set GAIN so the -18 dB LED lights on peaks, -12 dB rarely. Average signal at -20 dB.
  4. Phase [Ø] only if two mics on one source sound thin (kick, snare). See if turing invert phase on makes it sound muddy.
  5. HPF on in Selected Channel view. Start with the table value below.
  6. EQ in small moves. Mainly remove/cut bad sounding frequencies.
  7. Fader up, and balance against the full band. Listen to each souce in context with other audio sources. (Vocals loud and center)
  8. Check overall volume, keep all faders around Unity (0 dB) for the most fader percision. Adjust the master output for volume of the overall room volume.

HPF: raise it until the unwanted lows clear, then back off if the source loses body. Toggle it on and off to check.

EQ tips

  • Cut before you boost. Cut the mud instead of boosting everything else.
  • Sweep to find it. Boost 6 dB with a narrow Q, find the worst spot, then turn it into a 2–4 dB cut.
  • Narrow Q to cut, wide Q to boost.
  • Keep moves to 1–4 dB. Needing 8 dB or more means check the mic position or gain.
  • Judge in the full mix. Solo sounds can mislead you.
  • Make space. If two parts fight, cut a little from the less important one. Bypass the EQ to check it still helps.

Volume targets by part of service

Speaking 65–75 dB, music about 75–85 dB. Measure with an SPL meter or app set to A-weighting, slow response, in the same spot every week. Over 85 dB is too loud.

Compressor settings

A compressor turns down the loud parts so the level stays even. Turn on Dynamics 1 in Selected Channel view, then use the settings below.

SettingWhat it doesTip
ThresholdThe level where compression starts. Sound below it is left alone. Lowering the threshold means more of the signal gets turned down.Lower it until the gain reduction meter shows 3–6 dB on the loudest parts.
RatioHow hard the signal above the threshold is turned down. At 4:1, a signal 4 dB over the threshold comes out only 1 dB over.2:1 to 3:1 is gentle. Higher ratios sound heavier.
AttackHow fast the compressor reacts once the signal crosses the threshold. Slow lets the front of a note through (more punch). Fast catches it (smoother, but can sound dull).10–30 ms for vocals.
ReleaseHow fast the compressor lets go after the signal drops below the threshold. Too fast sounds jumpy. Too slow keeps squashing the next note.50–150 ms for vocals.
KneeHow gradually compression starts around the threshold. Hard knee kicks in suddenly and is easier to hear. Soft knee eases in and sounds more natural.Soft knee for vocals and speaking.
Gain (makeup)Raises the level after compression to make up for what was turned down.Match the level with the compressor on and off, so louder doesn't fool you into thinking it sounds better.
Gain reduction (GR) meterShows how many dB the compressor is turning down at any moment.3–6 dB on peaks is gentle. If it is always deep, raise the threshold.

Setting the threshold

  • Drums: have the drummer hit that drum solidly, over and over. Lower the threshold until the meter averages about -6 dB on each hit.
  • Vocals: while the singer sings out, lower the threshold until the meter reads about -6 dB at their loudest moments.
  • Bass: during a loud part of a song, set the threshold so the meter averages about -6 dB.
  • Gain of +6 dB makes up for about 6 dB of reduction. If you are reducing less, lower the gain. Compare at equal loudness.
  • Electric guitar, keys and overheads usually need little or no compression. Start with it off.
  • Set gain and HPF first, then the compressor.
  • Start with the instrument table above, then adjust by ear.
  • Compare with it bypassed at the same loudness. If it isn't better, turn it off.
  • Watch for feedback. Compression also brings up quiet sounds, so open mics can ring sooner.
